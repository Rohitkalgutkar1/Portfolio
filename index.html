I'm Rohit Kalgutkar. I design wiring harnesses, the bundles of wire, terminals and connectors that give a vehicle its nervous system.

My work sits where an electrical diagram becomes a physical product. A harness has to carry the right current to the right place, fit the space the vehicle leaves for it, and be something a production line can build the same way every time. Getting all three right is the job.

I started as a trained ITI electrician in 2015 and went on to a diploma in electrical engineering. At Aptiv Components I spent close to two years validating terminal crimp quality, the small joint every circuit depends on. Today I design harnesses at Yazaki India, working on Honda and Maruti Suzuki accounts.

Having seen the work from the crimp up, I design with the factory floor in mind.
